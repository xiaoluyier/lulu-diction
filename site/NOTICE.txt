Lumei Diction Studio

French/Italian speech and included MP3 samples: Piper SIWIS (fr_FR) and Paola (it_IT), https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices . SIWIS French Speech Synthesis Database (CC BY 4.0), https://datashare.is.ed.ac.uk/handle/10283/2353 ; OHF-Voice Paola dataset (CC0), https://github.com/OHF-Voice/voice-datasets . Audio generated from lyric text. ONNX Runtime Web, Microsoft, MIT license. Model cards are distributed in the voices directory.

Speech recognition: vosk-browser 0.0.8 by ccoreilly et al., Apache-2.0.
https://github.com/ccoreilly/vosk-browser
Vosk French and Italian small acoustic models 0.22, Alpha Cephei, Apache-2.0.
https://alphacephei.com/vosk/models
The model archives retain their original readme files; only packaging was changed.

Speech/IPA: eSpeak NG, GPL-3.0-or-later. Emscripten distribution 0.3.5 from Echogarden.
https://github.com/echogarden/espeak-ng-emscripten
https://github.com/echogarden/espeak-ng/tree/fork
Distribution modification: unused language dictionaries were omitted. tools/assets.mjs in the source repository reproduces this packaging. License: vendor/ESPEAK-LICENSE.txt.

Icons: Lucide, ISC and MIT. See vendor/LUCIDE-LICENSE.txt.
Source and asset build instructions: https://github.com/xiaoluyier/lulu-diction
